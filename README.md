## Overview

**School Management System** is a modern school management platform designed to help private schools manage their academic, administrative, and communication workflows from a single system.

The platform is being designed as a **multi-tenant SaaS**, with strong domain boundaries, server-side authorization, historical academic integrity, and a web-first experience that can support future mobile clients.

The initial target market is **private schools in Morocco**, with support for:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- RTL
- LTR

The project is intentionally designed as a long-term product rather than a collection of independent CRUD screens.

---

# Product Vision

The goal is to provide schools with one reliable platform for managing the complete academic lifecycle of their students while giving each user a clear experience based on their role and responsibilities.

The system aims to simplify:

- Student management
- Teacher management
- Parent relationships
- Academic structure
- Curriculum management
- Class management
- Grade management
- Attendance tracking
- Homework
- School announcements
- Notifications
- Historical academic records

The product prioritizes:

> **Correctness → Security → Maintainability → Simplicity → Scalability**
